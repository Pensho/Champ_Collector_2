Trait specific information like "Escalated at Infractions" does not belong in the skill_data. That info will ever only affect holders of the trait and so the trait should manage that information.

Probably the same with "Bonus Damage on Trait Condition", but this is at least possible to utilize by many. However it still smells.

I also notice that there seem to be many fields now that affect conditional damage fractions, so why not just have a "Conditional Damage Fraction" field that can be handled in code instead of separate fields? I suppose that will require some sort of look-up on use to check the right criteria though. But that criteria could be a callable field in the skill then maybe?

Also, there is no "Physical" or "Magical" damage. In descriptions of skills those words are not needed.

Adding a "buff_duration_overrides" seems like a bad bandaid solution, It would be better if each entry in buffs contain a corresponding duration.
