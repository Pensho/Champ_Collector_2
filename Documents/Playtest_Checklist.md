# Playtest Checklist

What to watch for when players play: how they fare against content, and whether the game shows what
is happening. Each entry names what to observe; the doc that owns the design gets updated from the
answer, and the entry is deleted.

## Encounters

### Hue-Eater (`Encounter_Design_Document.md` 2.2)

- **Gathered Light frequency.** It rarely completes in phase 1 and hits very hard. Do players meet it
  often enough to learn it, without it landing so often the fight turns punishing?
- **Beam luck.** Do the beams' rolls, and her hitting herself while Hexed, feel fair? If not, hide
  the odds: without Hexed beams hit champions more often, with Hexed almost never.
- **Beam readability.** Without the "Passes through" text, can players tell a beam passed through
  her?
